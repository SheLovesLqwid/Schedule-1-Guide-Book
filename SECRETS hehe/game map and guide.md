## 💉 **Drug Production**

### 💣 Methamphetamine (Meth)

**Unlocking Production**:  
Unlock meth production by advancing to the Westville area. You need to offer free samples to NPCs like Jerry Montero or Meg Cooley to achieve Friendly Status. This will lead to Shirley Watts, who supplies the essential Pseudo ingredient. 🌟

**Production Process**:
| Step | Action                                     | Time (In-Game) | Time (Real)  |
|------|--------------------------------------------|----------------|--------------|
| 1    | Purchase Chemistry Station & Lab Oven      | N/A            | N/A          |
| 2    | Acquire Pseudo, Acid, and Phosphorus       | N/A            | N/A          |
| 3    | Combine ingredients in Chemistry Station   | 8 hours        | 8 minutes    |
| 4    | Transfer liquid meth to Lab Oven           | 6 hours        | 6 minutes    |
| 5    | Package crystal meth for distribution      | N/A            | N/A          |
