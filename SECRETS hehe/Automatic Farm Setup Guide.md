## 1. **Farm Setup and Requirements** 🚜

To begin, you'll need to set up the essential infrastructure for a **fully automated farm**. Below is a detailed list of the required items for the farm setup:

### **Required Items**:
- **15 Large Storage Racks** – Essential for holding various farm products.
- **8 Drying Racks** – For drying farmed items (e.g., herbs, plants).
- **16 Suspension Racks** – To keep materials suspended for further processing.
- **16 Full Spectrum Grow Lights** – Provide optimal growth conditions for plants.
- **16 Pots** – Used by botanists to grow and nurture plants.
- **10 Beds** – For worker resting areas to ensure efficiency.
- **2 Cauldrons** – Used for chemical processing of plant materials.
- **2 Lab Ovens** – For curing or baking materials.
- **3-9 Brick Presses** – Needed for shaping and processing materials after mixing.
- **8 Mixing Stations MK2** – For mixing ingredients and creating complex items.

## 2. **Worker and Task Management** 👨‍🌾

### **Worker Types**:
1. **Botanists (3)**:
   - Botanists manage the **growing process** and prepare plants for the drying racks.
   - Each botanist is assigned **2 pots**.
   - Once plants are ready, they are transferred to the **Drying Racks** for further processing.

2. **Chemists (3)**:
   - Chemists manage the **chemical processes** needed for mixing ingredients.
   - This includes using **Cauldrons**, **Lab Ovens**, and **Mixing Stations** to turn raw materials into final products.

3. **Handlers (3)**:
   - Handlers are responsible for **moving items** between stations: from drying racks to cauldrons, from ovens to mixing stations, and from mixing stations to brick presses.
   - They also manage inventory and ensure **storage is always replenished**.

---

## 3. **Automated Farming Process** 🤖

The **goal** is to make your farm **fully automated**, reducing the need for human intervention and ensuring that everything flows smoothly.

### **Step 1: Automated Plant Growth**
- **Botanists** plant seeds into **Pots** and transfer them to the **Drying Racks** once grown.
- Set up **grow lights** around your farm to simulate optimal growing conditions for your plants. These lights ensure plants grow at their maximum potential.

### **Step 2: Drying Process Automation**
- **Drying Racks** are used to dry the harvested plants. Botanists will place materials here.
- **Handlers** automatically move dried plants from the racks to **Cauldrons** for chemical processing.

### **Step 3: Chemical Processing**
- **Cauldrons** are used for chemical treatments that prepare plants for mixing.
- After processing, the items are sent to **Lab Ovens** to undergo curing or baking.

### **Step 4: Mixing and Creation**
- Once materials have been processed, they are transferred to **Mixing Stations MK2**. Here, the materials are mixed in **specific sequences** to create your final product.
- Ensure that **Handlers** are assigned to transport materials in and out of the mixing stations to maintain a steady flow.

### **Step 5: Pressing Process**
- After mixing, the product is sent to **Brick Presses** for shaping or final processing.
- Once processed, **Handlers** will transport the finished products to **storage**.

## 4. **Optimizing Workflow with Automation** ⚙️

To make the entire farm process automatic, follow these strategies:

### **Worker Assignments and Routing**:
1. **Botanists**:
   - Botanists should be assigned to **specific pots** and drying racks.
   - Set up routes to automatically transfer items from **Pots** to **Drying Racks** when plants are ready.

2. **Handlers**:
   - Set up **12 automated routes** for handlers to move materials between **drying racks, cauldrons, ovens, mixing stations, and brick presses**.
   - Make sure to define **specific storage** points for the materials to be picked up and transferred at the right times.

3. **Chemists**:
   - Chemists should be assigned to **mixing stations** in a **1:1 ratio**, ensuring they work in sequence from **Cauldron → Oven → Mixer**.
   - Use **automated paths** for moving materials between the **mixing stations** in the correct order.

### **Storage Optimization**:
- Set up **automated stock replenishment** systems to ensure that materials don’t run out.
- Designate specific **storage areas** for materials at each stage of the process (e.g., dried materials, mixed materials, processed items).

## 5. **Resource and Material Flow** 📦

### **Material Movement Strategy**:
- All materials should be tracked from the moment they enter the system until they are processed and stored.
- **Handler routes** are crucial in this process. Automate the transfer of materials in a **sequential flow** from one station to another.

### **Automated Shelving**:
- Use **shelving units** to keep materials organized. Assign **handlers** to stock these shelves regularly.
- Ensure **materials are automatically moved** from **drying racks** to **storage** once dried, from **cauldrons** to **mixing stations**, and from **mixing stations** to **brick presses**.

## 6. **Scaling Your Operation** 📈

As your farm grows, you may want to expand the system. Here are tips for scaling:

### **Adding More Stations**:

### **Automation Enhancements**:
- Increase the number of **automated routes** and **storage areas** to handle larger quantities of materials.
- Use **advanced automation tools** to streamline tasks like **restocking shelves** and **material transfer**.
